Our approach

A home should feel like the people who live in it.

We design rooms for real life: natural materials, soft light, pieces with a story, and space to gather. Every project begins with how you want a room to feel, and ends with a home that feels unmistakably yours.

Services

Ways to work with us.

From a single room to the whole house, with design and custom carpentry under one roof.

  • Full-service interior design

    From the first walkthrough to the last styled shelf, we guide layout, materials, finishes and furnishings, for one room or the whole home.

  • Room refreshes & styling

    Paint, lighting, textiles and a fresh edit of what you already own, so a room feels new without starting over.

  • Design consultations

    A focused session in your home or virtually, ending with a clear direction you can carry out at your own pace.

  • Custom built-ins & millwork

    Built-ins, paneling, accent walls and timber beams, designed in-house and built in our Utah workshop to fit your space exactly.

    A specialty: heirloom playhouses & bunk rooms.

Design + build

Designed and built under one roof.

Jessica leads the design, shaping each room around how you live and what you love. Cody brings it to life in our Utah workshop, building the built-ins, paneling and beams that make a room feel finished.

With design and craftsmanship at the same table, every detail is drawn to be built, and the vision stays intact from the first sketch to the final install.

Process

How a project comes together.

  1. 01

    Consult

    We walk the space with you, talk through how you live, and listen for how you want each room to feel.

  2. 02

    Design

    A cohesive plan for layout, color, materials, lighting and furnishings, plus drawings for any custom pieces.

  3. 03

    Build & install

    Custom millwork is built in our workshop and installed with care, alongside the rest of the room coming together.

  4. 04

    Style

    The finishing layer: art, textiles, accessories and the small details that make it feel lived in from day one.
