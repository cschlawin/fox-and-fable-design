# Editing the Fox & Fable website

This guide is for Jessica. You don't need to know any code. You edit the website in **Pages CMS**, a free
website editor that works in your web browser, on a computer or a phone.

> **Before your first sign-in:** Cody has to finish a one-time setup (give you access and connect Pages CMS to the
> website). Until then, the sign-in steps below won't show the Fox & Fable site.

---

## 1. Sign in

1. Go to **https://app.pagescms.org**.
2. Choose **Sign in with GitHub**, then use your own GitHub username and password. (If Cody invited you by email
   instead, open the invitation email and use the sign-in link in it.)
3. Choose the **fox-and-fable-design** website.
4. The menu on the left lists what you can edit: **Home page**, **Room tours**, **Shop**, **Journal posts**,
   **About page**, **Social links**, **Work-with-me band**, **Footer & contact** and
   **Page titles & share text**.

Bookmark the page so you can find it again.

## 2. Add a room photo

**To add a photo to a room that's already on the site:**

1. Open **Room tours** and find the room in the **Photo tours** list. Each one shows its caption,
   so it's easy to spot.
2. Open **More photos (open when someone clicks the cover)** and press **Add an entry**.
3. Under **Photo**, press the photo box and **Upload** the picture from your computer or phone.
   (Phone photos straight from your camera roll are fine, including iPhone HEIC photos.)
4. Fill in **Photo description (for screen readers)**: one sentence describing what's in the room, for people who can't see the photo.
   For example: *"Pantry shelves painted deep green with glass jars and baskets."*
5. Set **Label** to *Photo*, *Detail*, *Before* or *Design (rendering)*.
6. Press **Save**.

**To add a whole new room:**

1. In **Room tours**, scroll to the end of the **Photo tours** list and press **Add an entry**.
2. Choose the **Room type** (this decides which filter button it appears under), and fill in the **Room label**
   (for example *Pantry*), **Caption** and **Location** (city and state only, never a street address).
3. Upload the **Cover photo** and write its **Photo description**.
4. Pick a **Tile shape**: *Tall (most rooms)* or *Wide*.
5. Add any extra photos under **More photos**, then press **Save**.

You can drag entries up and down to change the order on the page.

To change one of the four rooms on the **Home page**, open **Home page → Room tours preview → Rooms shown on the home page**.

## 3. Edit a caption

1. Open the page the caption is on. Room captions are in **Room tours**; family photo captions are in
   **About page → Our family → Photo captions**.
2. Click into the text box, change the words, and press **Save**.

Most text on the site works the same way: headings, the welcome message on the home page, your story on the
About page, and the footer text in **Footer & contact**.

## 4. Add a shop link

1. Open **Shop**.
2. Under **Favorite finds**, press **Add an entry** (or open one of the placeholder cards that say *[Product name]* and replace them).
3. Fill in:
   - **Category**: for example *Lighting* or *Paint*.
   - **Product name** and **Store**.
   - **Product link**: paste the full web address of the product, starting with `https://`.
     (If you leave it empty, the card shows "[Link]" so you know it still needs one.)
   - **Product photo** and **Photo description** (optional).
   - **Short note** (optional): for example *"The exact color in our pantry."*
4. Press **Save**.

Shop links open in a new tab and are marked as sponsored links, which is what search engines expect for affiliate links.

Your **Instagram, Pinterest and TikTok** links are under **Social links**.

## 5. Write a journal post

1. Open **Journal posts** and press **Add an entry**.
2. Fill in the **Title**, **Date** and **Category** (for example *Color* or *Styling*).
3. Upload a **Cover photo** and write its **Photo description**.
4. Write a one-sentence **Summary**. Search engines show it, and so do texts and social posts that share the link.
5. Write the post in the **Post** box. The toolbar works like a word processor: headings, bold, italics, lists,
   links and photos.
6. **Coming soon:** while this switch is **on**, the home page shows a "Coming soon" teaser card and the post
   isn't linked. Switch it **off** when the post is ready, and the card becomes a link to the full post.
7. Press **Save**.

The home page shows the newest three posts. You can change that number under **Home page → Journal section → How many posts to show**.

## 6. What happens after you press Save

1. Pages CMS saves your change to the website's files on GitHub. Every save is kept, so nothing is ever lost and
   any change can be undone.
2. GitHub then tidies any new photos automatically:
   - it **removes hidden location (GPS) and camera details**,
   - fixes the colors and rotation,
   - shrinks large photos to at most 2000 pixels so pages load quickly,
   - and makes smaller copies for phones.
3. GitHub rebuilds the website and publishes it. Your change is usually live in **2 to 5 minutes**.
   Refresh the page to see it. If it doesn't show up, wait a minute and refresh again.

If something looks wrong, don't worry: tell Cody and he can undo it.

## Good habits

- **Never write the kids' names** anywhere: captions, photo descriptions, file names or journal posts.
  Before uploading, check photos for name signs, wall decals, house numbers and readable mail.
- **Ask clients first** before posting photos of their home, and never include their names or street addresses.
- **Always fill in the photo description.** It helps visitors who use screen readers, and helps search engines.
- Text in **[square brackets]** is a placeholder. Replace it with the real thing when you have it.
- Videos must be **MP4** files. Phone videos can be large, so ask Cody to help with new videos.
- Some parts aren't in the editor on purpose: the logo, colors and layout, the family photos on the About page,
  the contact form, and the menu. Ask Cody if you want to change any of those.
