Meet the Designer

Jessica Designer & Founder · Fox & Fable

Hi, I’m Jessica! I’m a wife, mom of three, and the designer behind Fox & Fable.

In 2023, we moved into our builder-grade home nestled along the Wasatch Mountains. I fell in love with the location and views, but I knew I wanted to add character and make the space feel more intentional and uniquely ours.

Since then we have been transforming our home room by room, with plenty more projects still to come. Did I mention my husband, Cody, is the talent behind my ideas — the one who somehow turns all of my “what if we…” ideas into reality.

Through Fox & Fable, I get to combine my passion for design with our love for building, creating custom spaces that feel elevated, intentional, and built for real life — from indoor playhouses and bunk rooms to full home renovations.

I’ve shared glimpses of our projects over the years, but I’m excited to finally share more of the process, inspiration, and finished spaces here.

Thanks for following along!
