Design approach

Rooms with warmth, character and purpose.

Every Fox & Fable room starts with how it should feel, then comes together through palette, materials and the layers that make a house feel like home.

  • A considered palette

    Colors chosen for the light in each room, from soft neutrals to a moody, full-room color drench.

  • Natural materials

    Stone, wood, brass and linen that bring texture and warmth.

  • Layered, not cluttered

    Lighting, textiles, art and styled shelves added in thoughtful layers.

  • Made for real life

    Storage and layouts that work as well as they look, for the way you actually live.
