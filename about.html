Meet Jessica

Hi, I’m Jessica! I’m a wife, mom of three, living in the greater Salt Lake City area.

In 2023, we moved into our builder-grade home nestled along the Wasatch Mountains. I fell in love with the location and views, but I knew I wanted to add character and make the space feel more intentional and uniquely ours.

Since then we have been transforming our home room by room, with plenty more projects still to come. Did I mention my husband Cody is the talent behind my ideas, and the one who somehow turns all of my “what if we…” ideas into reality.

I’ve shared glimpses of our renovations over the years, but I’m excited to finally share more of the process, inspiration, and finished spaces here.

Thanks for following along.

Playful Spaces. Timeless Memories.

Built like architecture. Loved like a storybook.

Every Fox & Fable structure begins with a story: a world designed around your child, your room, and your home's architecture, then crafted locally in our Utah workshop.

  • Artisan craftsmanship

    Solid hardwoods, real joinery, and hand-finished details made to be passed down.

  • Non-toxic sustainability

    Responsibly sourced wood and low-VOC, child-safe paints and finishes.

  • Child-centered safety

    Rounded edges, secure anchoring, and a final safety check on every install.

  • Architectural integrity

    Designed to your room's proportions so it reads as part of the house, not an add-on.

Marked by hand. Each piece will carry our brand, pressed into the wood with a custom iron, so the story of who made it stays with it for the next generation.
